WHO WE ARE

MORE THAN A BAND.

THE MALANG is a live music band built around energy, emotion and a genuine love for music.

From intimate acoustic sessions to high-energy live stages, we bring our own sound and character to every performance. Our goal is simple — create moments that people remember long after the music stops.

Whether it's a college festival, wedding, private celebration or a major event, THE MALANG turns every stage into an experience.
